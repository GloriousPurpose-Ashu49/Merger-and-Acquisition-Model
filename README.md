# Strategic M&A Transaction Model

## Project Overview
- Developed an M&A financial model to analyse the acquisition of a smaller competitor by a larger company.
- Prepared a five-year forecast (2025–2029).
- Evaluated deal structure, funding requirements, synergies, debt repayment and EPS accretion/dilution.

## Key Deal Details
- Offer price: **$36 per share**, representing a 20% premium.
- Total equity offer value: **$376 million**.
- Payment structure: 55% stock and 45% cash.
- Total funding requirement: **$459 million**.
- Goodwill created: **$260 million**.

## Key Findings
- **Revenue Growth:** Combined revenue is projected to increase from $2.03 billion in 2025 to $2.48 billion by 2029.
- **Profitability:** EBITDA margin is expected to improve from 23.3% to 27.2%.
- **EPS Growth:** Consolidated EPS is projected to increase from $3.01 to $4.66.
- **Synergy Benefits:** Expected EBITDA contribution from synergies increases from $49 million to $188 million.
- **Debt Repayment:** The $100 million term loan is scheduled to be fully repaid by 2029.
- **Deal Performance:** Corrected EPS accretion is estimated at 10.9% in the first year and 52.4% by 2029.

## Important Observations
- Without synergies, the deal results in 4.4% EPS dilution in the first year.
- If revenue synergies generate a normal 21% EBITDA margin instead of being treated as 100% profit, first-year accretion falls to 1.1%.
- The deal's success depends heavily on achieving the planned cost savings and revenue synergies.
- The model highlights the importance of checking assumptions before making an acquisition decision.
